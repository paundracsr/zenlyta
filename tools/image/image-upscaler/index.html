Free Online Image Upscaler

zenlyta's image upscaler helps you enhance image resolution and quality using advanced interpolation algorithms. Increase the size of your images while maintaining sharpness and detail.

No account is required. All processing happens locally on your device, ensuring your images remain private.

How to Upscale an Image

  1. Select a JPG, PNG, or WebP image from your device.
  2. Choose the scale factor: 2×, 4×, or 8×.
  3. Adjust sharpness and smoothness sliders for fine-tuning.
  4. Click "Upscale Image" and preview the result.
  5. Download the upscaled image.

Why Upscale Images?

Upscaling images can improve quality for printing, display on high-resolution screens, or enhance low-resolution photos. It's also useful for restoring old images or preparing images for professional use.

Privacy-First Image Processing

Frequently Asked Questions

Is zenlyta's image upscaler free?

Yes. zenlyta's core browser tools are designed to be free to use without requiring an account.

Are my images uploaded?

Supported client-side image processing happens directly in your browser. The selected image does not need to be uploaded to a zenlyta file-processing server.

What image formats are supported?

The tool supports JPG, PNG, and WebP images.

What upscaling methods are used?

The tool uses bicubic interpolation for smooth scaling and nearest-neighbor for pixel-perfect enlargement. Sharpness and smoothness settings help fine-tune the result.

Can I upscale images on my phone?

The tool is designed to work in modern desktop and mobile browsers. Performance may vary depending on the device, browser, and image size.
