Free Online Image Overlay Tool

zenlyta's image overlay tool lets you blend two images together directly in your browser. Place a logo, watermark, texture or second photo on top of a base image with full control over opacity, position, scale, rotation and blend modes.

No account is required. Both images are processed locally — they never leave your device.

How to Overlay Images

  1. Select a Base Image (background) and an Overlay Image (top layer).
  2. Choose a quick position or drag the overlay on the preview.
  3. Adjust opacity, scale, rotation and blend mode — preview updates live.
  4. Fine-tune X/Y offset for pixel-perfect placement.
  5. Choose output format (PNG/JPG/WebP) and download.

Blend Modes Explained

Normal simply places the overlay on top with opacity. Multiply darkens, Screen lightens, Overlay combines both for contrast. Difference and Exclusion create artistic inversions. Experiment live to find the perfect look for double-exposure, watermarks, or creative composites.

Privacy-First Processing

zenlyta is designed around a client-side, privacy-first architecture. For supported local processing, the selected images are processed directly in your browser rather than being uploaded to a remote server.

Learn more on the System Architecture page and read our Privacy Policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is zenlyta's image overlay free?

Yes. zenlyta's core browser tools are designed to be free to use without requiring an account.

Are my images uploaded?

Supported client-side image processing happens directly in your browser. The selected images do not need to be uploaded to a zenlyta file-processing server.

What blend modes are available?

Normal, Multiply, Screen, Overlay, Darken, Lighten, Color Dodge, Color Burn, Hard Light, Soft Light, Difference, Exclusion, Hue, Saturation, Color and Luminosity — all rendered live on canvas.

Can I drag the overlay?

Yes. After loading both images, drag the overlay directly on the preview canvas. You can also use the position presets and X/Y offset sliders for precise control.

What output formats are supported?

PNG (preserves transparency), JPG and WebP. Quality is adjustable for JPG/WebP.
