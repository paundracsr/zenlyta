Free Online Color Picker

zenlyta's color picker helps you extract colors from any image. Simply upload an image and click anywhere to get the exact color in HEX, RGB, HSL, and CMYK formats.

No account is required. All processing happens locally on your device, ensuring your images remain private.

How to Pick Color from Image

  1. Select a JPG, PNG, or WebP image from your device.
  2. Click anywhere on the image to pick a color.
  3. View the color in HEX, RGB, HSL, and CMYK formats.
  4. Click on any color value to copy it to your clipboard.

Why Use a Color Picker?

Color pickers are essential for designers, developers, and creatives who need to extract exact colors from images for branding, web design, graphic design, and more.

Privacy-First Image Processing

zenlyta is designed around a client-side, privacy-first architecture. For supported local processing, your image never leaves your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is zenlyta's color picker free?

Yes. zenlyta's core browser tools are designed to be free to use without requiring an account.

Are my images uploaded?

Supported client-side image processing happens directly in your browser. The selected image does not need to be uploaded to a zenlyta file-processing server.

What color formats are supported?

The tool supports HEX, RGB, HSL, and CMYK color formats.

What image formats are supported?

The tool supports JPG, PNG, and WebP images.

Can I pick colors on my phone?

The tool is designed to work in modern desktop and mobile browsers. On touch devices, tap the image to pick a color.
