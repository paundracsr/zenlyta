Free Online Photo Colorizer

zenlyta's photo colorizer helps you add color to black and white or faded images directly in your browser. Adjust the hue shift, saturation, and color intensity to achieve the desired effect.

No account is required. All processing happens locally on your device, ensuring your images remain private.

How to Colorize a Photo

  1. Select a JPG, PNG, or WebP image from your device.
  2. Adjust the hue shift and saturation sliders to control the color tone.
  3. Set the color intensity to blend the effect.
  4. Click "Apply Colorize" and preview the result.
  5. Download the colorized image.

Why Colorize Photos?

Colorizing old black and white photos can bring them to life and add a new dimension to your memories. It's also useful for artistic projects or restoring faded images.

Privacy-First Image Processing

zenlyta is designed around a client-side, privacy-first architecture. For supported local processing, your image never leaves your device.

Learn more about how zenlyta works on the System Architecture page and read our Privacy Policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is zenlyta's photo colorizer free?

Yes. zenlyta's core browser tools are designed to be free to use without requiring an account.

Are my images uploaded?

Supported client-side image processing happens directly in your browser. The selected image does not need to be uploaded to a zenlyta file-processing server.

What image formats are supported?

The tool supports JPG, PNG, and WebP images.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is required to use the core photo colorization functionality.

Can I colorize photos on my phone?

The tool is designed to work in modern desktop and mobile browsers. Performance may vary depending on the device, browser, and image size.
