Free Browser-Based Image Rotator

zenlyta’s Rotate Image tool allows you to fix sideways or upside-down photos quickly. Smartphone camera orientation tags (EXIF orientation) sometimes cause images to display sideways on web pages; this utility corrects rotation easily.

How to Rotate an Image

  1. Upload your JPG, PNG, or WebP photo into the workspace.
  2. Use quick preset buttons (+90°, -90°, 180°) or adjust the Custom Angle slider.
  3. Click Apply & Render Rotated Image to construct the canvas.
  4. Download your updated rotated image directly.

Presets & Custom Rotation

Clockwise (+90°)Turns portrait photos landscape or vice versa
Flip Upside Down (180°)Inverts orientation by 180 degrees
Custom Angle (0°-360°)Fine-tune alignment for tilted photos

Frequently Asked Questions

Why did my photo upload sideways?

Smartphones store rotation preferences inside EXIF metadata, which some browsers ignore. Rotating your image fixes the pixel orientation permanently.

Can I rotate custom fine angles like 45 degrees?

Yes. You can use the Custom Rotation Angle slider to rotate anywhere between 0° and 360°.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Canvas operations are executed locally within your browser context.
