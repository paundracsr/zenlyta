Free Online Background Blur Tool

zenlyta's background blur tool helps you automatically detect and blur image backgrounds while keeping the main subject sharp. Perfect for portrait photos, product images, and creative projects.

No account is required. All processing happens locally on your device, ensuring your images remain private.

How to Blur Image Background

  1. Select a JPG, PNG, or WebP image from your device.
  2. Adjust blur intensity and edge sensitivity sliders.
  3. Choose blur method and detection method.
  4. Click "Apply Background Blur" and preview the result.
  5. Download the blurred image.

Why Blur Backgrounds?

Blurring backgrounds can help focus attention on the main subject, create professional-looking portraits, or add artistic depth to your photos. It's useful for photography, e-commerce, and social media content.

Privacy-First Image Processing

zenlyta is designed around a client-side, privacy-first architecture. For supported local processing, your image never leaves your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is zenlyta's background blur tool free?

Yes. zenlyta's core browser tools are designed to be free to use without requiring an account.

Are my images uploaded?

Supported client-side image processing happens directly in your browser. The selected image does not need to be uploaded to a zenlyta file-processing server.

How does the background detection work?

The tool uses edge detection algorithms to identify the main subject and blur the background while keeping the subject sharp.

What image formats are supported?

The tool supports JPG, PNG, and WebP images.

Can I blur backgrounds on my phone?

The tool is designed to work in modern desktop and mobile browsers. Performance may vary depending on the device and image size.
