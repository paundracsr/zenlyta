Free Online Favicon Generator

zenlyta's favicon generator helps you create professional favicons for your website. Generate multiple sizes and formats from any image directly in your browser.

No account is required. All processing happens locally on your device, ensuring your images remain private.

How to Generate Favicon

  1. Select a JPG, PNG, or WebP image from your device.
  2. Choose the favicon size (or select "All Sizes").
  3. Select output format (PNG or ICO).
  4. Choose background color (transparent or custom).
  5. Click "Generate Favicon" and preview the result.
  6. Download individual sizes or all at once.

Why Use a Favicon?

Favicons help users identify your website in browser tabs, bookmarks, and history. They're essential for branding and professional website appearance.

Privacy-First Image Processing

zenlyta is designed around a client-side, privacy-first architecture. For supported local processing, your image never leaves your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is zenlyta's favicon generator free?

Yes. zenlyta's core browser tools are designed to be free to use without requiring an account.

Are my images uploaded?

Supported client-side image processing happens directly in your browser. The selected image does not need to be uploaded to a zenlyta file-processing server.

What sizes are available?

The tool generates favicons in 16×16, 32×32, 48×48, 64×64, 128×128, and 256×256 sizes.

What image formats are supported?

The tool supports JPG, PNG, and WebP images as source.

Can I generate favicons on my phone?

The tool is designed to work in modern desktop and mobile browsers. Performance may vary depending on the device and image size.
