Free Online PNG Compression Utility

zenlyta's PNG compressor optimizes PNG graphics directly in your web browser. Transparent PNG images, icons, logos, and screenshots can be reduced in size without uploading them to an external server.

How to Compress PNG Files

  1. Select a PNG image from your device.
  2. Adjust the quality slider to find your preferred balance of size and fidelity.
  3. Click "Compress PNG" to execute client-side compression.
  4. Preview the size savings and download your compressed PNG image.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is zenlyta's PNG compressor free?

Yes, it is 100% free with no registration or usage limits.

Does PNG compression remove transparency?

No. Transparency (alpha channel) is preserved during client-side compression.

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

No, all compression is performed directly in your browser canvas engine.
