Free Online Image Cropper

zenlyta's image cropper helps you cut out a specific rectangular region from supported images directly in your browser. Use the interactive crop area, choose preset aspect ratios, or freely drag to define the selection.

No account is required to use the tool. Select an image, drag on it to create a crop rectangle, preview the dimensions, and download the result.

How to Crop an Image

  1. Select a JPG, PNG, or WebP image from your device.
  2. Drag on the image to create a crop area.
  3. Optionally choose a preset ratio (1:1, 4:3, etc.) or use Free crop.
  4. Preview the selected dimensions.
  5. Click "Crop Image" and download the result.

Why Crop Images?

Cropping is useful for removing unwanted areas, focusing on the subject, or changing the aspect ratio. It can also be used to prepare images for specific layouts or social media platforms.

Privacy-First Image Processing

zenlyta is designed around a client-side, privacy-first architecture. For supported local processing, the selected image is processed directly in your browser rather than being uploaded to a zenlyta file-processing server.

This approach reduces the need to transmit personal or sensitive image files to a remote processing service.

Learn more about how zenlyta works on the System Architecture page and read our Privacy Policy for additional information.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is zenlyta's image cropper free?

Yes. zenlyta's core browser tools are designed to be free to use without requiring an account.

Are my images uploaded?

Supported client-side image processing happens directly in your browser. The selected image does not need to be uploaded to a zenlyta file-processing server.

What image formats are supported?

This tool supports JPG, PNG, and WebP images.

Can I use preset ratios?

Yes, choose from Free, 1:1, 4:3, 16:9, 3:2, and 9:16.

Can I crop images on my phone?

The tool is designed to work in modern desktop and mobile browsers with touch support. Performance may vary depending on the device, browser, and image size.
