Free Online Passport Photo Maker

zenlyta’s Passport Photo Maker allows you to format and prepare passport-sized photos online in seconds. Whether you need standard 2x2 inch photos for US passports or 35x45 mm photos for Schengen visas and European documents, this utility handles sizing and cropping instantly inside your web browser.

How to Create a Passport Photo

  1. Upload a clear portrait photo taken against a plain background.
  2. Select your desired passport dimension standard (e.g. US 2x2" or EU 35x45mm).
  3. Choose a background fill color if needed.
  4. Click Generate Passport Photo to render the finalized document photo.
  5. Download the high-resolution passport photo directly to your device.

Standard Passport & ID Requirements

Most government agencies require passport photos to adhere to strict dimensional and composition guidelines. Standard guidelines generally mandate:

  • A front-facing headshot with a neutral facial expression.
  • Even lighting without strong shadows on your face or background.
  • Standard background colors such as pure white, light gray, or specific country-mandated blue/red shades.
  • Appropriate head height filling roughly 60% to 70% of the overall frame height.
