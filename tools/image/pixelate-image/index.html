Free Online Pixelate Image Tool

zenlyta's pixelate image tool lets you pixelate photos for privacy or style with adjustable pixel size, mosaic, and posterize modes.

No account is required. All processing happens locally on your device, ensuring your images remain private.

How to Pixelate an Image

  1. Select an image file from your device.
  2. Preview the image and adjust the pixelation settings before applying the effect.
  3. Choose the output format (PNG, JPG, or WebP).
  4. Adjust the pixel size and choose the style that fits your design.
  5. Click "Apply Pixelate" and preview the result.
  6. Download the pixelated image.

Why Use Pixelation?

Pixelation is useful for hiding sensitive information, creating retro visual styles, or reducing detail for previews.

Privacy-First File Processing

zenlyta is designed around a client-side, privacy-first architecture. For supported local processing, your Image never leaves your device. The parsing and rasterization happen entirely in your browser via Canvas API.

Learn more about how zenlyta works on the System Architecture page and read our Privacy Policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is zenlyta's pixelate image tool free?

Yes. zenlyta's core browser tools are designed to be free to use without requiring an account.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Image parsing and frame extraction happen directly in your browser using pixelate. Your Image never leaves your device.

What output formats are supported?

You can export frames as PNG (lossless, transparent), JPG (smaller, adjustable quality), or WebP.

Can I pixelate on my phone?

Yes, you can extract all frames, first 10/20, or a custom range with step (e.g., every 2nd frame).

Can I download the result?

Yes, you can download all frames as a ZIP archive or trigger individual downloads for each frame.
