Free Online Rounded Corners Tool

zenlyta’s Round Corners tool lets you soften the edges of any photo by applying smooth, anti-aliased rounded corners. It is ideal for profile avatars, product cards, presentation slides, social media posts, and web UI elements where sharp rectangular edges feel too harsh.

No account is required. Upload an image, drag the radius slider, preview the result instantly, and download the final file with transparent or colored corners.

How to Round Image Corners

  1. Upload a JPG, PNG, or WebP image from your device.
  2. Adjust the Corner Radius slider or choose a preset (Sharp, Medium, Large, Circle).
  3. Check the live preview — corners update in real time.
  4. Choose export format: PNG for transparent corners or JPG with a custom background color.
  5. Click Apply Rounded Corners and download the result.

Why Round Image Corners?

Rounded corners make images feel softer, more modern, and more approachable. They are a staple of contemporary UI design — from app icons and avatars to cards and banners. Rounding corners can also help images blend better into layouts with rounded containers, reducing visual tension between sharp photos and soft UI elements.

For circular avatars, set the radius to 50% (Circle preset) on a square image to create a perfect circle.

Privacy-First Image Processing

zenlyta is designed around a client-side, privacy-first architecture. Your image is processed directly in your browser using the Canvas clipping API — it is not uploaded to a zenlyta file-processing server.

This keeps your personal photos private and ensures fast processing. Learn more on the System Architecture page and read our Privacy Policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I round image corners?

Upload your image, move the radius slider to your desired roundness, and click Apply. The tool uses canvas clipping to create smooth anti-aliased corners.

Will rounding reduce image quality?

No. The image is rendered at its original resolution. Corners are anti-aliased for smooth edges without pixelation.

Can I make a circular image?

Yes. Use the Circle preset (50% radius) on a square image to create a perfect circle. For non-square images, the result will be an oval with fully rounded ends.

What happens to the corner background?

PNG and WebP exports keep corners transparent, so you can place the image on any background. JPG exports fill corners with the color you choose.

Are my images uploaded?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your image never leaves your device.
