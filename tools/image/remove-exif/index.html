Why You Should Remove EXIF Data

Modern digital cameras and smartphone cameras automatically embed Exchangeable Image File (EXIF) metadata into every photo you capture. This hidden metadata often contains precise GPS location coordinates, capture dates and timestamps, camera hardware serial numbers, device model details, and lens settings.

When sharing photos online, on social media platforms, or via messaging apps, this embedded information can compromise your personal location privacy and identity. Stripping EXIF metadata before sharing ensures your location and device info remain confidential.

How to Remove Metadata Online

  1. Upload your JPG or PNG image to zenlyta Remove EXIF.
  2. Click the Strip EXIF Metadata button.
  3. The tool redraws a pristine, metadata-free image on an HTML5 canvas locally in your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is EXIF metadata?

EXIF stands for Exchangeable Image File Format. It is standard metadata embedded into photo files containing camera settings, timestamps, and geolocation tags.

Does stripping EXIF metadata reduce image quality?

No. The visual pixel data remains fully intact while unnecessary hidden header attributes are completely discarded.

Are my images kept private?

Yes. Processing is completed client-side inside your browser. No files are transferred to external servers.
