Free Online Black & White Converter

zenlyta’s Black & White tool transforms any color photo into timeless monochrome directly in your browser. Choose between Grayscale for smooth, natural tones with 256 shades of gray, or Threshold for bold, high-contrast pure black and white — perfect for artistic prints, photocopy effects, or stencil art.

No account is required. Upload an image, pick your mode, preview instantly, and download the result as PNG or JPG.

How to Convert to Black & White

  1. Upload a JPG, PNG, WebP, or GIF image from your device.
  2. Choose Grayscale for smooth tones or Threshold for pure black & white.
  3. If using threshold, adjust the slider to control the black/white cutoff.
  4. Check the live side-by-side preview.
  5. Click Convert & Download and save as PNG or JPG.

Grayscale vs Threshold — Which to Choose?

Grayscale uses the luminosity formula (0.299R + 0.587G + 0.114B) to preserve detail and depth — ideal for portraits, landscapes, and classic photography. Threshold forces every pixel to pure black or white based on your cutoff, creating stark, graphic results great for logos, stamps, photocopy art, and high-contrast designs.

Both modes preserve original dimensions and sharpness while completely remapping the color palette to monochrome.

Privacy-First Image Processing

zenlyta is designed around a client-side, privacy-first architecture. Your image is converted directly in your browser using the Canvas pixel API — it is not uploaded to a zenlyta file-processing server.

This keeps your photos private and ensures instant processing even on slow connections. Learn more on the System Architecture page and read our Privacy Policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between grayscale and threshold?

Grayscale keeps 256 shades of gray for smooth, photographic tones. Threshold forces every pixel to pure black or white based on a cutoff — creating high-contrast, graphic results.

Will converting reduce image quality?

No. The operation preserves the original width, height, and sharpness. The result is exported as PNG (lossless) or JPG.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. All pixel manipulation happens locally in your browser via the Canvas API.

How does the threshold slider work?

In threshold mode, each pixel’s brightness is compared to your value (0–255). Brighter than threshold becomes white, darker becomes black. Lower values produce more white, higher values more black.

Do I need an account?

No account is required. The tool works on desktop and mobile browsers for free.
