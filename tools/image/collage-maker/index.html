Free Online Collage Maker

zenlyta's collage maker helps you create beautiful photo collages by combining multiple images. Perfect for social media posts, photo albums, and creative projects.

No account is required. All processing happens locally on your device, ensuring your images remain private.

How to Create a Collage

  1. Upload up to 6 images (JPG, PNG, or WebP).
  2. Choose a layout (2 Grid, 3 Grid, 4 Grid, 6 Grid, etc.).
  3. Select background color for your collage.
  4. Adjust spacing and border radius as needed.
  5. Click "Create Collage" and preview the result.
  6. Download your collage as PNG.

Why Use a Collage Maker?

Collages are perfect for showcasing multiple photos in one frame. Use them for social media, photo albums, mood boards, or creative presentations.

Privacy-First Image Processing

zenlyta is designed around a client-side, privacy-first architecture. For supported local processing, your images never leave your device.

Learn more about how zenlyta works on the System Architecture page and read our Privacy Policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is zenlyta's collage maker free?

Yes. zenlyta's core browser tools are designed to be free to use without requiring an account.

Are my images uploaded?

Supported client-side image processing happens directly in your browser. The selected images do not need to be uploaded to a zenlyta file-processing server.

How many images can I use?

The collage maker supports up to 6 images in various layouts.

What image formats are supported?

The tool supports JPG, PNG, and WebP images.

Can I create collages on my phone?

The tool is designed to work in modern desktop and mobile browsers. Performance may vary depending on the device and image sizes.
