About Zenlyta

Your free online toolkit for everyday file tasks — built for privacy, speed, and simplicity.

Zenlyta currently provides 50 free online image tools for compression, conversion, resizing, and editing — all directly in your browser.

No uploads. No servers. Just your browser and your images. Document, developer, text, and calculator tools are coming soon.

Privacy-First by Design

We believe your files are your business. That's why many of our tools process data locally on your device using client-side JavaScript. Files are never uploaded to our servers unless explicitly required (and we clearly disclose that).
