Where Your Support Goes

Zenlyta is developed with a simple goal: build useful tools that remain accessible to everyone.

Donations help support the ongoing work required to maintain the project, including development, testing, hosting, domain costs, infrastructure, security improvements, browser compatibility, and future tools.

We do not require donations to use Zenlyta. The core experience is intended to remain accessible without requiring users to pay.

No Pressure. Just Support.

You don't need to donate to use Zenlyta.

If our tools have helped you finish a task, save time, protect sensitive files, or simply made your day a little easier, supporting the project is one way to help us keep building.

Whether it's $5, $25, or simply sharing Zenlyta with someone who might find it useful — we appreciate your support.

Thank you for being part of Zenlyta.
