Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Here's how Zenlyta protects your data.

📅 Last updated: August 27, 2026

1. File Processing

Zenlyta is designed with a privacy-first architecture. For most tools, all file processing happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your files are never uploaded to our servers or any third-party service during these operations.

Tools that process files locally include:

  • All 27 Image Tools — compress, resize, crop, convert, optimize, edit, inspect, and generate images directly in your browser.
  • All 10 PDF Tools — compress, merge, split, convert, rotate, extract, page numbers, metadata
  • All 14 Developer Tools — JSON, QR, Hash, Base64, JWT, regex, UUID, SQL, HTTP, cron, color picker, CSS generators, URL/HTML entity
  • All 7 Text Tools — case, word count, diff, duplicate remover, lorem ipsum, markdown preview, TTS
  • All 5 Calculators — age, BMI, discount, loan, percentage

Exception: If a specific tool requires server-side processing (e.g., complex OCR or AI-based features), we will explicitly state this on the tool's page and request your consent before uploading any data.

2. Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share the content of your files. We may collect anonymous usage statistics (e.g., which tool is used most often) to improve our services, but this data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

3. Cookies

We may use minimal cookies solely for functionality (like remembering your theme preference). We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies without your explicit consent.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not share your data with third parties. If we integrate external services (like analytics) in the future, we will update this policy and provide you with the option to opt-out.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us.
