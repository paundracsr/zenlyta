Privacy Policy

Learn how Zenlyta handles your files, privacy, analytics, cookies, and third-party services.

📅 Last updated: August 27, 2026

1. Image Processing

Zenlyta is built with a privacy-first architecture. Our image tools are designed to process supported images directly in your web browser whenever client-side processing is technically supported.

Zenlyta currently provides 50 free image tools for common image processing, conversion, editing, optimization, and image-related tasks.

  • Compression & Optimization — compress and optimize JPG, PNG, and WebP images to reduce file size.
  • Resize, Crop & Transform — resize, crop, rotate, flip, split, and transform images directly in your browser.
  • Format Conversion — convert supported formats including JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, AVIF, SVG, EPS, TIFF, and ICO.
  • Image Editing — adjust brightness, blur images, remove backgrounds, add watermarks, pixelate images, invert colors, and apply black-and-white effects.
  • Image Creation — create collages, memes, favicons, sprite sheets, overlays, and image-based PDF files.
  • Image Analysis & Metadata — inspect EXIF metadata, remove private metadata, extract colors and palettes, and recognize text from images using OCR.
  • Image Utilities — calculate aspect ratios, convert images to Base64, decode Base64 data, and optimize SVG files.

Privacy by Design: When using a client-side image tool, your files never leave your device. Processing takes place directly in your browser without uploading the selected image to a Zenlyta processing server.

Processing Notice: If a specific tool requires server-side processing or transmission to an external service, this will be clearly disclosed on the tool's page before the relevant data is transmitted.

2. Data Collection

Zenlyta does not intentionally collect or store the contents of images processed by its client-side image tools.

When you select an image for a client-side tool, the selected file is processed locally by your browser and is not uploaded to a Zenlyta processing server.

Zenlyta may collect limited technical or usage information through services such as website analytics. This information may include general information about page visits, browser type, device type, approximate geographic information, or which pages and tools are accessed.

Analytics information is used to understand how the website is used, improve our tools, monitor performance, and improve the overall user experience.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

Zenlyta may use browser technologies such as cookies, local storage, or similar technologies to provide basic website functionality.

These technologies may be used to remember interface preferences, support website features, maintain settings, or improve the user experience.

Third-party services integrated into Zenlyta, including analytics and advertising providers, may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own privacy policies and terms.

4. Analytics

Zenlyta may use analytics services to understand website traffic and usage patterns.

Analytics may provide information such as page views, referral sources, device and browser information, general geographic information, and interactions with website pages or tools.

This information helps us understand which image tools are useful, identify technical issues, improve website performance, and develop future features.

Analytics providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

5. Advertising

Zenlyta may display advertisements to help support the development, maintenance, hosting, and continued availability of its free image tools.

Advertising providers may use cookies, advertising identifiers, or similar technologies to provide, measure, and improve advertisements according to their own policies.

Zenlyta does not provide advertising partners with the contents of images processed through its client-side image tools.

6. Third-Party Services

Zenlyta may use third-party services to operate, analyze, secure, improve, or monetize the website. These services may include hosting providers, analytics services, advertising networks, content delivery services, or other technical infrastructure providers.

Third-party providers may process certain technical information according to their own privacy policies, terms of service, and applicable laws.

Client-side image tools are designed so that supported image files can be processed locally in your browser without uploading those files to a Zenlyta processing server.

7. Data Security

Zenlyta takes reasonable measures to maintain a privacy-focused architecture and protect the website and its users.

Client-side processing reduces the need to transmit supported image files to a remote processing server. This approach is intended to minimize unnecessary exposure of user files during image processing.

However, no website, browser, network, or internet service can guarantee absolute security. Users are encouraged to keep their operating system, browser, and security software reasonably up to date.

8. External Links

Zenlyta may contain links to external websites, services, or resources that are not operated by us.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, availability, or content of external websites and services.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy and terms of any third-party service before providing personal information or using that service.

9. Children's Privacy

Zenlyta is a general-purpose website providing browser-based image tools. We do not intentionally collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the website, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

10. Privacy Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Zenlyta introduces new tools, features, integrations, advertising services, analytics services, or changes to its data practices.

The latest version of this policy will be published on this page. The date shown at the beginning of this policy indicates when it was last updated.

11. Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how Zenlyta handles data, please contact us .
